The MemeCapes Launchpad runs on Meteora's audited bonding-curve program on Solana. Coins are made and owned by the people who launch them, not by MemeCapes. Anyone can launch a coin, so check who is behind one before you buy. Most new coins lose most of their value: only put in what you are happy to lose. Nothing here is investment advice.
