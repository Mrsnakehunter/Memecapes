# MemeCapes progress

_Last updated: October 8, 2026_

## Where to look

- **Live game:** [play.html](https://mrsnakehunter.github.io/Memecapes/play.html), the current public version.
- **Preview:** [preview.html](https://mrsnakehunter.github.io/Memecapes/preview.html), which has all the new 3D art. It goes live once it's approved.

## Done (in the preview)

- **119 3D building and prop models** made for the world, each sized to match the characters.
- **All 8 meme towns** have their own themed houses and landmarks: Wow Landing, Pepe's Pond, Stonks City, Diamond Hands, Rugpull Ridge, HODL Heights, Moon Landing and Chad Falls. Gigachad Town has its own building set.
- **Town details:**
  - Themed lamps, signposts in every town and a herald's sign where players start.
  - Market stalls, wells, fountains and crates.
  - Docks at the fishing spots and lily pads on the pond.
- **Countryside:**
  - Boulders, logs, stumps and mushrooms.
  - Haystacks and scarecrows at the farms.
  - Ruins, graves, a camp with tents and campfires, and mine carts.
  - A dwarven gate at the Copium Mountains.
  - A lunar lander and craters at Moon Landing.
- **Copper and tin rocks** use real 3D models, and the Meme Caves have a rocky mine entrance.
- **The Meme Ring** and the **Meme Coin icons** for stacks of 1 up to 10M+.
- **Themed banks** in Wow Landing, Pepe's Pond, Stonks City and Diamond Hands.

## In progress

- **Themed banks and general stores for the rest of the towns,** plus a 3D blacksmith forge and cooking range.
- **New inventory pictures for all 99 items:** weapons, tools, food, gems, potions, masks, helmets, armour and capes.
- **3D weapons and tools in the character's hand,** including swords, clubs, staffs, axes and pickaxes.

## Built and waiting for a spot in the world

- **Lava zone set:** obsidian hut, furnace cave and lava bridge.
- **Stone and wooden bridges, walls and fences.**
- **Tutorial island buildings:** bakery, camp, quest hut, shrine, vault, training yard and a portal dock.
